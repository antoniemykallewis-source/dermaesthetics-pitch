Finding 1 of 3

Give patients one clear address.

What we saw

The homepage questions section lists Foothills Boulevard. The footer lists East Roseville Parkway.

Why it matters

New patients may not know which location to visit.

Our fix

Confirm the current address. Match the website, booking page and Google Business Profile.

Finding 2 of 3

Show who will care for them.

What we saw

The homepage mentions specialists and nurses without naming the person patients will meet.

Why it matters

People want to know who is treating them.

Our fix

Add the person’s name, role and real photo. Film a short, friendly introduction.

Finding 3 of 3

Let people learn before they book.

What we saw

Google’s website and booking buttons both open Vagaro.

Why it matters

New patients may want to read about treatments first.

Our fix

Send the website button to your site. Keep Vagaro for booking, with clear links from each treatment.

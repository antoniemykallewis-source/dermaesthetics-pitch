What we found / 1 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

01

Google skips your treatment story.

Its website and booking buttons both lead to Vagaro. Patients comparing care reach a service list before learning about your providers and approach. Booking works; destination choice is the issue. See the Google profile ↗

02

Two addresses put visits at risk.

The questions section says Foothills Boulevard; the footer says East Roseville Parkway. Patients can head to the wrong location, miss a visit or choose another clinic. See the two addresses ↗

03

Patients cannot meet the providers.

Homepage nurses and specialists are unnamed, with no embedded provider video. A real person and clear credentials help patients feel ready to book. This concerns the homepage only. See the provider section ↗

What we found / 2 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

04

Treatment choices get lost at booking.

Every homepage treatment button opens the same Vagaro service list. Finding the treatment again adds another chance to stop booking. Booking was not completed; deep links need checking. See the booking destination ↗

05

Give search a useful introduction.

Homepage source lacks a meta description. A clear treatment-and-location summary could make your search result easier to understand before someone clicks. Google chooses its displayed snippet. See the website ↗

06

Review totals need a clear source.

The combined five-star total lacks a nearby platform breakdown or date. Patients checking other sources cannot easily reconcile the proof, creating uncertainty when they should feel ready to book. See the review claim ↗

What we found / 3 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

07

Treatment claims need precision.

The homepage says FDA-approved and safe for all skin types; Aerolase says FDA-cleared. Broad claims can set expectations an individual consultation cannot support. Clinical review needed; no unsafe-care conclusion. See the treatment wording ↗

08

8.5 seconds in the phone test.

October 20’s slow 4G homepage simulation measured this delay. Interested patients can leave before the treatment message or booking button. Lab LCP, not every visit; no real-visitor data. See the phone test ↗

How we would help / 1 of 2

HERE’S THE FIX. The work we’d do.

01

Give Google two clear destinations.

Link the website button to your branded treatment information and keep the booking button for Vagaro. Check both paths on a phone.

02

Make your location unmistakable.

Confirm the current address and align the questions section, footer, Google profile and booking details. Review priority directory entries before changing them.

03 + 07

Let patients meet the real care team.

Publish approved names, credentials and genuine portraits. Film provider introductions, consultation answers and aftercare. Use accurate treatment wording and case context approved by the clinical team.

How we would help / 2 of 2

HERE’S THE FIX. The work we’d do.

04

Carry treatment choices into booking.

Check supported Vagaro deep links and preserve the selected service where possible. Explain when a consultation is needed, with an obvious contact fallback.

05 + 06

Build local pages with clear proof.

Add useful search descriptions and treatment pages that answer patient questions. Link original review profiles with source, location and date rather than an unexplained aggregate.

08

Bring the first screen forward.

Prioritize the main image, reduce unnecessary scripts and improve caching. Retest the phone load while keeping the booking route easy to reach.
